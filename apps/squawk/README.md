# Squawk

A friendly wake-up alarm for iPhone, built on Apple's AlarmKit. One simple alarm by
default, with extra rings and wake-up checks when you need them, and a penguin named
Squawk who cheers you on.

- [PRODUCT.md](PRODUCT.md): what it is and why (approved)
- [DESIGN.md](DESIGN.md): screens, flows, motion and copy, with [mockups](design/mockups/squawk.html)
